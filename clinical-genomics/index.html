Pharmacogenomics & CPIC Drug Metabolism

Genetic polymorphisms in drug-metabolizing enzymes and transporters (DMET) dictate individual therapeutic efficacy and adverse event susceptibility:

  • CYP1A2 (rs762551): Regulatory variant modulating hepatic cytochrome P450 1A2 expression; stratifies rapid vs. slow caffeine and theophylline clearance.
  • CYP2C9 (rs7900194, rs28371685): Modulates metabolism of narrow therapeutic index oral anticoagulants (Warfarin) and NSAIDs.
  • NAT2 (rs1801280): N-acetyltransferase 2 polymorphism associated with slow acetylation phenotype and elevated drug toxicity risks during isoniazid therapy.
  • COMT (rs4680, Val158Met): Catechol-O-methyltransferase variant regulating prefrontal dopamine enzymatic clearance ("Worrier vs. Warrior" cognitive traits).
🔍

ClinVar Curation & Quality Architecture

Our python pipeline (`clinvar_annotator.py`) parses GRCh37/GRCh38 VCF coordinate conversions, resolves rsIDs, and classifies assertions into standardized ACMG tiers:

  • Coverage: 609,387 raw Illumina OmniArray SNPs processed with zero missing barcode dropouts.
  • Filtering Logic: Restricts reportable subsets to variants with review status ≥ 1 star (criteria provided).
  • Personal Genomic Privacy: Raw individual microarray callsets remain protected in the Private Core Vault; summary stats are rendered securely on client.
