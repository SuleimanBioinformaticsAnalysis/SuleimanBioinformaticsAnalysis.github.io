How to Submit an Access Application

To expedite institutional review, please include the following details in your email request:

  • Principal Investigator & Institution: Academic or research organization affiliation.
  • Intended Research Purpose: Validation, replication, meta-analysis, or peer-review assignment.
  • GitHub Username: For collaborator invitation to SuleimanBioinformaticsAnalysis.
  • Data Security Agreement: Acknowledgment that raw personal SNP data will remain confidential.
